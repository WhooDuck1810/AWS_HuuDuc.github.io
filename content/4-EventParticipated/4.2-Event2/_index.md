# Summary Report: “Career Orientation & AWS Architecture”

### Event Overview
- **Event Name:** Career Orientation & AWS Architecture
- **Date & Time:** June 13, 2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee

---

### Key Topics & Highlights

1. **Industry Insights (Data & DevOps)**
   - Dispelling common misconceptions surrounding Data Engineering and Cloud DevOps careers.
   - Understanding market demands and essential skill sets for cloud engineers.

2. **5-Step Career Growth Model**
   - Structured roadmap for transitioning from junior engineering roles to senior architecture and leadership.
   - Emphasizing continuous learning and hands-on cloud project building.

3. **Corporate Culture & Mindset**
   - Exploring the **No-Blame Culture** in multinational corporations (MNCs).
   - Encouraging root-cause analysis and blameless post-mortems over fault assignment.

4. **AWS Scalable Architecture Case Study**
   - Deep dive into designing highly available and scalable cloud systems on AWS.
   - Analyzing load balancing, auto-scaling groups, and multi-region database strategies.
